## Display order

The strip starts with current workplaces, followed by larger established brands and institutions, then smaller companies and community organizations. Order is fixed between visits.

Closeup and Layer5 sit near the start of the larger-company group, alongside MTN, Scania, United Nations Volunteers, Mastercard, and Access Bank, as requested by the owner.

Antrak Express follows Old Mutual. InnoCode and InnoKit are separated in the strip, after Pageline Concepts and Teens In AI respectively, as requested by the owner.

Meshery follows Synlab, separated from Layer5 as requested by the owner.

White-heavy marks and text entries are separated by coloured marks, including at the loop seam. Transparent outer margins are trimmed without altering artwork, and each mark has an optical display height rather than a fixed-width slot. All logo dimensions are scaled uniformly to 80.5% of their individually tuned sizes (a 15% increase from the previous 70% scale). Visible logo bounds have a consistent 128px horizontal gap. The scroll remains slow and pauses on hover. Google was removed at the owner's request.

Scania has a wider optical allowance so its wordmark has comparable visual height to Access Bank's lettering. University of Ghana uses the owner's coloured-crest replacement.

The strip can also be scrolled infinitely in either direction with horizontal trackpad scrolling, touch swipes, mouse dragging, or the left/right arrow keys when focused. Automatic movement pauses during hover, keyboard focus, and manual interaction. Reduced-motion preferences disable automatic movement while preserving manual scrolling.
