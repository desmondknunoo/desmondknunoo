# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Employers and collaborators evaluating Desmond Kwame Nunoo's professional experience, capabilities, and work.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that presents Desmond's work and background and helps prospective collaborators understand his fit and get in touch.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The site is built with Next.js, React, and Tailwind CSS. The About page should bring together professional experience, education, certifications, and evidence of work.

## Evidence on Hand

- Experience, education, and certification details supplied by the user in screenshots.
- Bootcamp and Google Accra AI Community Centre visit story supplied by the user, with LinkedIn post and image links.
- Existing project portfolio content and imagery in `src/lib/work.ts` and `public/images`.
- User-provided credential links for the Gemini Prompt Design badge, IMUN Online Conference, and KNUST Entrepreneurship Clinic certificate.

## Product Principles

- Present professional background clearly and accurately.
- Connect claims to relevant work and credentials when links are available.
- Make it easy for employers and collaborators to contact Desmond.
